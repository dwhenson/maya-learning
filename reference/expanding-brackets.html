The rule

The term outside the bracket multiplies every term inside.

a(b + c) = ab + ac

The picture: area model

Minus inside: the minus sticks to its term

In 4(x − 3) the terms are x and −3. The 4 multiplies both:

4(x − 3) = 4x − 12

4 × (−3) = −12: four hops of −3 on a number line, or four rows of three red −1 tiles.

Factorising: expanding backwards

Find the highest common factor (HCF) of the numbers, put it outside, then divide each term by it.

6x + 15 = 3(2x + 5)

Check: expand it back. Then check the inside can't share anything more (2 and 5 can't), so it's fully factorised.

🔍 Detective trick: check your answer

Swap the letter for 10 on both sides of the = sign and work each side out. Same number → probably right. Different → definitely wrong.

Left: 3(10 + 4) = 42
Right: 3×10 + 12 = 42 ✓

Words

Expand: rewrite without brackets by multiplying out. Term: one part of an expression, separated by + or −, like 3x or 12.
