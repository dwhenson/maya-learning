Stretch the triangle

2 min

How to play: drag the pink corners to make any triangle you like. Watch the three angles, and the total underneath.

Balance it

4 min
⭐ The big idea An equation is like a balance: both sides weigh the same. To find x, do the same thing to both sides until x is on its own.

Undo the + or − first, then undo the × by dividing. Remember 3x means 3 × x.

How to play: tap the move to do to both sides. Solve 4 to get 4 stars ⭐

🔺

Triangle puzzles

5 min

Now put it together. Three steps: add the angles, make it = 180, then solve.

How to play: drag each angle into the equation (or tap it), pick the tidy version, then solve on the balance.

Remember it

Three last questions. Tap the answer you think is right. No scrolling back up! 💪

A triangle has angles 50°, 70° and x. What is x?

  1. 50°
  2. 70°
  3. 60°
  4. 120°

Which is a good first step to solve 3x + 30 = 180?

  1. Take 30 from both sides
  2. Take 30 from the left only
  3. Add 30 to both of the sides
  4. Take 3 from both of the sides

A triangle has angles x, x and 2x. What is x?

  1. 30°
  2. 45°
  3. 60°
  4. 90°
