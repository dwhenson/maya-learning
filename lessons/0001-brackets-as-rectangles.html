Drag the number

3 min
⭐ The big idea The number outside the bracket multiplies every term inside.

A term is each separate bit inside the bracket. In 3(x + 4) the terms are x and 4.
And remember: 3 × x is written 3x.

How to play: drag the pink number onto a term inside the bracket (or just tap the term). Pick the right answer. Then do the other term. Get through 5 to collect 5 stars ⭐

🕵️

Spot the mistake

3 min

Some of these cards have been expanded correctly and some have a mistake. Can you tell which?

🔍 Detective trick Swap the letter for 10 on both sides of the = sign and work each side out. Same number? Probably right. Different? Definitely wrong!

2(x + 5) = 2x + 10 → left side 2 × 15 = 30, right side 20 + 10 = 30 ✓ they match

How to play: drag each card into ✅ Correct or 🙈 Oops (or tap a card, then tap a box).

Remember it

2 min

Three last questions. Tap the answer you think is right. No scrolling back up! Answering from memory is what makes it stick. 💪

What is 6(y + 3) expanded?

  1. 6y + 3
  2. 6y + 9
  3. 6y + 18
  4. y + 18

Why does 4(x + 3) give 4x + 12?

  1. The 4 multiplies both of the terms
  2. The 4 multiplies just the first term
  3. You add the 4 to each term
  4. The 4 and 3 are added together

Sam writes 7(x + 5) = 7x + 5. Use the detective trick with x = 10. What number does each side give?

  1. Left 75, right 75
  2. Left 105, right 105
  3. Left 70, right 75
  4. Left 105, right 75
