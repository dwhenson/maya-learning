Drag the number

3 min
⭐ The big idea The minus sign sticks to the term behind it. In 4(x − 3) the terms are x and −3.

So the 4 multiplies x and it multiplies −3. Just like the frog: 4 × (−3) = −12.

How to play: drag the pink number onto a term inside the bracket (or just tap the term). Pick the right answer. Then do the other term. Get through 5 to collect 5 stars ⭐

Spot the mistake

3 min

The most common slip is losing the minus. Can you catch it?

🔍 Detective trick Same as last time: swap the letter for 10 on both sides and work each side out. Same number? Probably right. Different? Definitely wrong!

Left: 4 × 7 = 28. Right: 40 − 12 = 28 ✓ they match

Three last questions. Tap the answer you think is right. No scrolling back up! Answering from memory is what makes it stick. 💪

What is 5(x − 4) expanded?

  1. 5x + 20
  2. 5x − 20
  3. 5x − 4
  4. 5x − 9

Why is 4(x − 3) equal to 4x − 12 and not 4x + 12?

  1. The 4 is always a minus
  2. You take 4 away from 12
  3. Minus beats plus every time
  4. The minus sticks to the 3

Sam writes 6(x − 2) = 6x − 2. Use the detective trick with x = 10. What number does each side give?

  1. Left 48, right 58
  2. Left 58, right 48
  3. Left 48, right 48
  4. Left 60, right 58
