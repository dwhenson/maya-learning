Negative numbers warm-up 🌡️

Hi Maya! 👋 A quick top-up on minus numbers, for whenever they feel wobbly. Do one part or both. About 10 minutes.

➡️ ➕ 🔮 ✖️
➡️

Part 1: Which way?

2 min

Think of a number line. Adding moves you right ➡️, taking away moves you left ⬅️. A negative number flips the direction:

5 + (−3) is the same as 5 − 3
Adding a negative ⬅️ moves left
5 − (−3) is the same as 5 + 3
Taking away a negative ➡️ moves right

How to play: drag each card into the way it moves (or tap a card, then tap a box).

➕

Adding and taking away

4 min

Work each one out and type the answer. Use the - key for minus, like −7. Get 6 right. If you slip, you'll see it on a number line.

🔮

Part 2: Positive or negative?

2 min

For times × and divide ÷ there's one rule. Look at the two signs:

Same signs → positive ➕
3 × 4 = 12
−3 × (−4) = 12
Different signs → negative ➖
−3 × 4 = −12
12 ÷ (−3) = −4

Remember the frog: 4 × (−3) is 4 hops of −3, so it lands on −12.

How to play: don't work out the answer yet! Just drag each card to say if its answer is positive or negative (or tap a card, then tap a box).

✖️

Times and divide

4 min

Now the full answer. Do the numbers first, then use the sign rule. Get 6 right.
