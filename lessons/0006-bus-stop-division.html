Bus stop division 🚌

Hi Maya! 👋 A neat way to divide big numbers in your head, one digit at a time. Learn it in 5 minutes, then play through the levels as often as you like.

🎬 👀 🎮 🧠
🎬

Watch first

4 min

Tap ▶ to play. Look at the little numbers that hop to the next digit.

👀

How it works

2 min
⭐ The big idea Go digit by digit, left to right. At each stop ask: how many fit? Write it on the roof. Anything left over hops onto the next digit, so 1 left over in front of a 9 makes 19.

How to play: tap Next step and watch the bus work out 896 ÷ 7.

🎮

Play the levels

as long as you like

How to play: the bus stops at the yellow digit. Tap the answer on the number pad (or type it). Finish 5 sums to clear a level and unlock the next.

⭐⭐⭐ for no slips. Stuck on a times table? Tap 🧮 to peek. Your stars and best times are saved on this device, so come back and beat them!

🧠

Remember it

2 min

Three questions. Use paper if you like. 💪

What is 72 ÷ 6?

  1. 11
  2. 13
  3. 12
  4. 14

What is 156 ÷ 4?

  1. 39
  2. 31
  3. 49
  4. 36

🚐 A school trip has 100 people. Each minibus takes 8. How many minibuses do they need?

  1. 12
  2. 12 r 4
  3. 8
  4. 13
