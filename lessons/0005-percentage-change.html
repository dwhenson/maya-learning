Stretch the price

4 min
⭐ The big idea The old price is 100%. Chop it into 10 blocks, so each block is 10%. Going up 20%? Add 2 blocks. Down 30%? Take 3 away.

How to play: drag the purple handle (or tap the bar) to stretch or shrink the price. Then pick the new price.

🧮

Find the pieces

4 min

Not every percentage is a whole number of 10% blocks. Build it from these pieces:

50%half it
10%÷ 10
5%half of 10%
1%÷ 100

Example: 15% of £60 → 10% is £6, 5% is £3, so 15% is £9.

How to play: type how much the percentage is worth, like 9 or 4.50. Get 5 right.

🏷️

Multiplier match

3 min
⚡ The shortcut Up 20% means you pay 100% + 20% = 120%, and 120% = 1.2. So just do price × 1.2.
Down 20% means you pay 100% − 20% = 80%, so do price × 0.8.

This number is called the multiplier. It's great with a calculator.

How to play: drag each card onto its multiplier (or tap a card, then tap a box).

✏️

Your turn: the shop

5 min

Work out each new price. Use blocks or the multiplier, whichever you like. A calculator is fine. Type the price, like 41.40.

Get 8 right to finish. If you slip, you'll see both methods.

🧠

Remember it

2 min

Three last questions. Tap the answer you think is right. No scrolling back up! 💪

A £50 game goes up by 10%. What's the new price?

  1. £5
  2. £55
  3. £60
  4. £45

Which multiplier takes 30% off a price?

  1. × 0.3
  2. × 1.3
  3. × 0.7
  4. × 0.03

Sam says an £80 jumper with 25% off costs £55. Is Sam right?

  1. Yes: 80 − 25 makes £55
  2. No: 25% is £20, so £60
  3. No: it should be £105
  4. No: it should be £64
