# Mission: Year 8 maths confidence (KS3)

## Why
Maya is in Year 8 and some topics taught in class haven't clicked yet. The course started with expanding brackets (single brackets were shaky, especially with negatives). On 2026-11-24 the parent widened it to the other Year 8 topics she needs: factorising, forming and solving equations, percentage change and short division. The goal for each topic is the same: a strong mental model of *why* it works, plus enough fun practice that she feels confident in class.

## Success looks like
- **Brackets:** explains with a picture (the area model) why 3(x + 4) = 3x + 12; expands single brackets with negatives; expands and simplifies; later, double brackets
- **Factorising:** takes out the highest common factor, e.g. 6x + 15 = 3(2x + 5), and checks by expanding
- **Equations:** forms an equation from angles in a triangle (they add to 180°) and solves it with "do the same to both sides"
- **Percentages:** increases and decreases amounts of money by a percentage, using 10% / 1% chunks and the multiplier (× 1.2, × 0.85)
- **Division:** divides confidently with the bus stop method, including carrying and remainders
- Spots and fixes the classic mistakes in each topic, and checks her own answers

## Constraints
- Maya works through lessons on her own in a browser; she sends feedback through the form on each page, and Claude reads it before planning the next lesson
- No deadline: depth of understanding over speed
- Short lessons with hands-on activities, practice and instant feedback

## Out of scope
- GCSE-level content (quadratic factorising, simultaneous equations, compound interest, reverse percentages) unless asked
