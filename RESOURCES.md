# Expanding Brackets Resources

## Knowledge

- [Video: Corbettmaths, "Expanding Brackets" (Video 13)](https://corbettmaths.com/2013/12/23/expanding-brackets-video-13/)
  Short, clear UK-teacher walkthrough of single brackets, with a matching worksheet and answers. Use for: the primary "watch this" source for single-bracket lessons.
- [Worksheet: Corbettmaths Expanding Brackets practice questions (PDF)](https://corbettmaths.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/expanding-brackets-pdf1.pdf)
  Graded practice with answers. Use for: extra paper practice once a lesson is done.
- [Video: Corbettmaths, "Expanding Two Brackets" (Video 14)](https://corbettmaths.com/2013/12/23/expanding-two-brackets-video-14/)
  Three methods for double brackets: distributive law, FOIL, grid. Use for: the double-brackets lessons later on (prefer the grid method; it is the area model).
- [Lesson: Oak National Academy, "Expand a term over a single bracket"](https://www.thenational.academy/teachers/lessons/expand-a-term-over-a-single-bracket-c8v62c)
  Free UK curriculum lesson that uses area models to explain expanding. Use for: backing up the area-model explanation. (Oak flagged some older lessons for replacement, so check the live site for newer versions.)
- [Lesson: Oak National Academy, "Checking and securing understanding of expanding a single bracket"](https://www.thenational.academy/teachers/programmes/maths-secondary-ks4-foundation/units/algebraic-manipulation/lessons/checking-and-securing-understanding-of-expanding-a-single-bracket)
  Uses algebra tiles and an area model "to see what is happening". Use for: the area-model and negatives lessons.
- [Interactive: GeoGebra, "Expanding Single Brackets – Algebra Tiles"](https://mat.geogebra.org/m/pgykvssb)
  Slider-driven rectangle showing a(x + b) as an area. Use for: hands-on play alongside lesson 1.
- [Interactive: Mathsbot virtual algebra tiles (Jonathan Hall)](https://mathsbot.com/manipulatives/tiles)
  Widely used UK virtual manipulatives. Use for: building brackets with tiles, especially negatives.
- [mathcentre: Expanding (removing) brackets](https://www.mathcentre.ac.uk/types/video/brackets/)
  University-run maths support videos. Use for: a second explanation if Corbettmaths doesn't land.
